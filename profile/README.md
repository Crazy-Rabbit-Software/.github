## How we build

- **Your data stays on your machine.** Everything our apps produce lives on your disk, in formats you can open without us. No account to start, no account to keep. Where a feature genuinely needs a server, like two people editing the same record in tabls, it stays scoped to that feature.
- **Buy a major version, own it.** One licence per major version, every update inside it free, forever. A new major version is a new decision, and the one you own keeps running either way.
- **Leaving has to be easy.** Open formats, exportable data, no proprietary vault.
- **Stack:** Tauri 2, Rust, SolidJS, TypeScript, WebGL.
